The resources below include datasets and computational tools developed across different projects in which I have participated. They are available for use by the research, education, and professional communities, subject to the license and usage terms provided with each resource.

<div class="academic-grid">
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Dataset · Cyber threat intelligence</p>
<h3><a href="https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/jt8yw5gdr2/1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Labeled Dataset of Cybersecurity Events from Dark Web and Surface Web Forums</a></h3>
<p>A collection of posts from Dark Web and Surface Web forums, with cybersecurity events classified by relevance. It supports research on threat intelligence, text mining, and machine-learning-based event detection.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Dataset · Malicious-content detection</p>
<h3><a href="https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/33kff5gb3h/1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DarkPT-BR</a></h3>
<p>A labeled collection of Brazilian Portuguese posts from Dark Web forums. It provides heuristic, manually reviewed, and model-generated labels for research on malicious-content detection and cyber threat intelligence.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Dataset · Cybercrime and judicial analytics</p>
<h3><a href="https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/tsg3xzgw6t/1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CyberJustice-TJSP</a></h3>
<p>A dataset of public judicial decisions from the São Paulo Court of Justice related to cybercrime. It includes full text, metadata, topic-modeling labels, and a manually annotated subset for supervised classification.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Software ecosystem · Cybersecurity training</p>
<h3><a href="https://github.com/rsmiani/gt-lfi-rnp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GT-LFI — Learn From Incidents</a></h3>
<p>An open-source ecosystem that turns real security incidents into operational knowledge and practical learning content through anonymization, AI-assisted classification, playbook generation, human validation, and gamified activities.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Platform · IoT security and management</p>
<h3><a href="https://iotedu.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GT IoTEdu</a></h3>
<p>A platform for registering, connecting, monitoring, and protecting IoT devices in institutional networks. It integrates device management, security monitoring, and automated response through firewall policies.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Research framework · Intrusion detection</p>
<h3><a href="https://github.com/kelsonc/genids-framework" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GenIDS Framework</a></h3>
<p>An experimental framework for studying how machine-learning intrusion-detection systems generalize across network-data domains. It includes flow extraction, dataset standardization, model training, inter-domain evaluation, and reproducibility resources.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Dataset and pipeline · DNS security</p>
<h3><a href="https://github.com/CborgesUFU/DNS-360-Dataset" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DNS-360 Dataset</a></h3>
<p>A curated dataset and experimental pipeline for detecting DNS-based data exfiltration over conventional DNS and DNS over HTTPS. It supports benchmarking, feature engineering, and machine-learning experiments.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Dataset · Sports analytics</p>
<h3><a href="https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/marcusmarchiori/informacoes-e-estatisticas-brasileirao-serie-a" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Informações e Estatísticas — Brasileirão Série A</a></h3>
<p>A dataset with detailed statistics from Brazilian Série A football matches between 2018 and 2023, including dates, rounds, home and away teams, stadiums, results, and other match information.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Software tool · IoT fingerprinting</p>
<h3><a href="https://github.com/GT-IoTEdu/sbrc26_fingerprint" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IoT-ID</a></h3>
<p>A prototype for identifying IoT devices through deterministic fingerprints derived from active probing and passive TCP/IP traffic analysis. It produces reproducible device identities that do not depend on IP addresses.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Research toolkit · Cyber threat intelligence</p>
<h3><a href="https://github.com/rsmiani/forum-threat-intelligence" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Forum Threat Intelligence</a></h3>
<p>A collection of research tools for forum discovery and collection, relational storage, text normalization, malicious-content scoring, temporal topic analysis, and cross-domain evaluation of threat-intelligence models.</p>
</article>

<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">Software tool · IoT experimentation</p>
<h3><a href="https://github.com/paulo-coelho/iot-sim" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IoT Simulator</a></h3>
<p>A configurable simulator for IoT devices, sensors, batteries, network conditions, and atypical events. It supports large-scale CoAP simulations, MQTT gateways, scheduled scenarios, and live monitoring.</p>
</article>
</div>
