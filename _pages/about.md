I am an Associate Professor and the Director of the [School of Computer Science (FACOM)](https://facom.ufu.br/) at the [Federal University of Uberlândia (UFU)](https://ufu.br/), Brazil. My work lies at the intersection of **cybersecurity, computer networks, data science, and artificial intelligence**, with an emphasis on methods that can be evaluated in realistic operational settings.

Current work
======

My current projects investigate AI-assisted cyber threat detection and response, resilient distributed data processing, hands-on cybersecurity education, and socially responsible applications of artificial intelligence. They include national and international collaborations with universities, public institutions, and industry. [Explore coordinated and collaborative projects →](/projects/)

Background
======

I earned a B.Sc. in Mathematics from the Federal University of São Carlos (2005), and M.Sc. (2009) and Ph.D. (2013) degrees in Electrical Engineering from the University of Campinas. During my doctorate, I spent six months as a visiting researcher at the CyberSecurity Quantification Laboratory at the [University of Maryland](https://www.umd.edu/), USA. I have been a faculty member at UFU since 2013.

I am a member of the Brazilian Computer Society and served on its Special Commission on Cybersecurity (CESeg) from 2024 to 2025. I also contribute to the research community through program committees, peer review, and event organization. [Read more about academic service →](/service/)
