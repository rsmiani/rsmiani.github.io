My research projects connect cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, computer networks, IoT, and data-intensive systems. Project titles are retained in Portuguese, as registered in my Lattes CV. Descriptions are provided in English for an international audience.

Projects as PI or Co-PI
======

<div class="academic-grid">
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2025–present · R&amp;D · PI</p>
<h3>GT-LFI - Sistema de Ensino-Aprendizagem, Gamificação e Classificação a partir de Incidentes</h3>
<p>Develops a gamified training platform for cybersecurity incident response. It uses real cases and AI-assisted analysis of incident data from partner CSIRTs.</p>
<p class="card-meta"><strong>Financial Support:</strong> RNP</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2025–present · Community outreach · PI</p>
<h3>CyberLab Udi</h3>
<p>Promotes cybersecurity awareness in Uberlândia through activities for children, teenagers, adults, and professionals, ranging from digital-safety education to technical training.</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2025–present · R&amp;D · Co-PI</p>
<h3>Preço Ágil Inteligência Digital para Compras Públicas Eficientes</h3>
<p>Applies AI and data analysis to make price research for public procurement faster, more consistent, and easier to audit.</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2025–present · Research · Co-PI</p>
<h3>CYBERGUARD: Inteligência Artificial para Detecção e Resposta de Ameaças Cibernéticas</h3>
<p>Studies AI-assisted threat detection and incident response by integrating SIEM and SOAR environments, with emphasis on scalability, generalization, and operational effectiveness in SOCs.</p>
<p class="card-meta"><strong>Financial Support:</strong> CNPq</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2024–2025 · Community outreach · PI</p>
<h3>ReCiber - Ressocialização de Cibercriminosos</h3>
<p>Developed educational and restorative-justice activities to support the rehabilitation and social reintegration of people investigated for cybercrime.</p>
<p class="card-meta"><strong>Financial Support:</strong> Ministério Público Federal</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2024–present · R&amp;D · Co-PI</p>
<h3>PesquisAI: Superando os desafios de Pesquisa de Preços em Contratações Públicas com aplicação inédita de Mineração de Dados e Inteligência Artificial nos mecanismos de busca</h3>
<p>Uses data mining and AI-enhanced search to improve price research and decision-making in public procurement.</p>
<p class="card-meta"><strong>Financial Support:</strong> FAPEMIG</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2022–2024 · Research · PI</p>
<h3>Identificação de ameaças de segurança usando mineração de dados de redes sociais e darkweb</h3>
<p>Developed methods to detect emerging cyber threats by mining social-media content and dark-web forums, producing actionable security alerts.</p>
<p class="card-meta"><strong>Financial Support:</strong> DataRisk</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2019–2025 · Research · PI</p>
<h3>Detecção de botnets IoT usando análise do tráfego de rede</h3>
<p>Investigated early detection of IoT botnets using network-traffic features and machine-learning models, including methods for identifying previously unseen botnet behavior.</p>
<p class="card-meta"><strong>Financial Support:</strong> FAPEMIG</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2014–2017 · Research · PI</p>
<h3>Uma abordagem para caracterização de ataques baseada na mineração de alarmes gerados por sistemas de prevenção de intrusão</h3>
<p>Applied data-mining methods to intrusion-prevention alerts to characterize attack behavior and make large alert collections easier to analyze.</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2014–2017 · Research · PI</p>
<h3>Análise Quantitativa de Incidentes de Segurança em Redes de Computadores</h3>
<p>Developed quantitative models linking IDS/IPS alerts to security incidents, with indicators for incident prediction and impact analysis.</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2013–2014 · R&amp;D · Co-PI</p>
<h3>GT-Plainc: Plataforma de Análise de Incidentes</h3>
<p>Developed a web platform that uses self-organizing maps to analyze security incidents and support operational decision-making.</p>
<p class="card-meta"><strong>Financial Support:</strong> RNP</p>
</article>
<article class="academic-card">
<p class="card-kicker">2009–2013 · Research · PI</p>
<h3>Utilização de métricas na elaboração de metodologias para o cálculo e aplicação de indicadores de segurança</h3>
<p>Developed standardized security indicators and studied relationships among metrics from multiple information-security data sources.</p>
</article>
